SaveMisu – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

In short: your budget stays on your phone. We have no accounts, no servers and no analytics, and we never receive your financial data. The only third party is Google AdMob, used for optional rewarded videos.

Who we are

SaveMisu (package com.mildmadgames.savemisu) is developed by Mild Mad Games, an individual developer. Contact: mildmadgames@gmail.com.

What data we collect

None. SaveMisu has no account or sign-in, no servers of ours, no cloud sync and no analytics SDK. We, the developer, never receive your data.

Data on your device

Everything you enter – expenses, income, fixed items, budgets, goals, savings, missions, stars, streak and Misu’s wardrobe – is stored only on your device, in a local database.

  • Export: you can export your data yourself (JSON/CSV) using the Android share sheet. Where it goes next is your choice.
  • Delete: you can erase everything from Settings (reset) or by uninstalling the app.

Ads (Google AdMob)

SaveMisu shows only optional rewarded videos (max 3 per day), served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK).

  • AdMob may collect device identifiers (advertising ID), IP address, approximate location and ad interaction data, for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention, and – only with your consent – for personalized ads.
  • In the EEA and UK, consent is collected with Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change your choices at any time in Settings → “Ad privacy options”.
  • No budget or financial data is ever passed to the ad SDK.

Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Notifications

SaveMisu can send an optional reminder if you haven’t opened the app for 2 days. It is generated on your device – there is no push server. You can turn it off in the app’s Settings or in Android settings.

Children

SaveMisu is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect children’s data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights over your personal data. Since we hold no personal data about you, requests concerning ad data should be sent to Google (see the links above). For any question, write to us at mildmadgames@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority – in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Changes

If we update this policy, the new version will be published in the app and at this same URL, with an updated date.

Contact
